Lesson 5

Common Mistakes in Prompting ⚠️

Learn about typical mistakes people make when prompting AI and how to avoid them.

Lesson progress 50%

1. Explanation

Even small changes in a prompt can lead to very different results. Here are the most common mistakes to avoid.

✗
Too Vague

Being too unclear makes it hard for the AI to understand your request.

✗
No Context

Without background information, the answer may be incorrect or incomplete.

✗
Too Long and Confusing

Very long prompts can confuse the AI and reduce the quality of the response.

✗
No Format or Structure

If you don't specify the format, the output may not be what you expected.

✅ Good Prompt Checklist
Clear and specific
Includes context
Concise
Well-structured
Defines the format

2. Examples

Compare a poor prompt with an improved version.

Poor Prompt
Improved Prompt
Why is the sky blue?
→
Explain why the sky appears blue during the day in simple terms for a high school student.
Write about history.
→
Write a short paragraph about the Industrial Revolution in England in the 18th century.
Give me ideas.
→
Give me 5 unique ideas for science fair projects related to renewable energy.
