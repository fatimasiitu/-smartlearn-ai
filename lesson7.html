Lesson 7

AI in Everyday Life

Discover how artificial intelligence is used in many tools and services we use every day.

Lesson progress 70%

1. Explanation

AI is already a part of our daily lives. Many apps and websites use AI to make things easier, faster, and more personalised.

Recommendation Systems
YouTube, Netflix, and Spotify recommend videos, movies, or songs you might like based on your history.

Virtual Assistants
Siri, Google Assistant, and Alexa understand your voice and help you with tasks.

Online Shopping
AI suggests products based on your browsing and purchase history.

Maps and Navigation
Google Maps uses AI to predict traffic and find the best routes.

2. Example

YouTube:

Recommends videos based on what you watch.

Google Maps:

Predicts traffic and offers the fastest route.

Gmail:

Filters spam emails and suggests smart replies.

Spotify:

Creates playlists based on your listening habits.
