Lesson 3

How AI Learns

Understand the basic process of how artificial intelligence learns from data.

Lesson progress 30%

1. Explanation

AI learns from data using a process called training.

Here's how it works:

  • 1
    Data Collection Large amounts of data are collected (text, images, numbers, etc.).
  • 2
    Pattern Recognition The AI looks for patterns and relationships in the data.
  • 3
    Model Building The AI builds a model based on those patterns.
  • 4
    Prediction The model uses what it learned to make predictions on new data.

The more quality data the AI sees, the better it becomes at making accurate predictions.
