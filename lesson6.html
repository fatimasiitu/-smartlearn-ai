Lesson 6

Why AI Makes Mistakes

Understand the limitations of AI and why it doesn't always give perfect answers.

Lesson progress 60%

1. Explanation

AI is powerful, but it can still make mistakes. Here are the main reasons:

Limited Knowledge
AI is trained on data available up to a certain time. It may not know new events.

Incorrect or Incomplete Data
If the training data is wrong or incomplete, AI can learn the wrong thing.

Ambiguous Questions
If your question is unclear, AI may misunderstand and give an incorrect answer.

AI Doesn't Truly Understand
AI predicts the best possible answer — it doesn't have human consciousness or real-world experience.

2. Example

Outdated Information:

AI might say a store is open on Sunday, but the schedule recently changed.

Wrong Data:

If data says "the sky is green", AI may learn and repeat that incorrectly.

Unclear Question:

If you ask a vague question, AI might guess and give a wrong answer.
