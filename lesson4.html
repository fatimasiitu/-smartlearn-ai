Lesson 4

Structure of a Good Prompt 💬

Learn how to write clear and effective prompts for better AI responses.

Lesson progress 40%

1. Explanation

A good prompt has four key parts that help the AI understand your request clearly.

The Structure:

  • Role Tell the AI who it should act as.
  • Task Describe what you want the AI to do.
  • Context Provide background information and details.
  • Format Specify the output format you need.
Prompt Structure
Role
Task
Context
Format
Example:
Role: You are a teacher.
Task: Explain photosynthesis.
Context: I am a high school student.
Format: Give the answer in simple bullet points.

2. Example

Let's look at a real example of a good prompt and how it helps the AI give a better answer.

Poor Prompt Why is the sky blue?
Result: The answer is too general and not helpful.
→
Good Prompt Role: Act as a computer science teacher.
Task: Explain AI.
Context: I am a beginner.
Format: Use 3 simple bullet points.
Result: The answer is clear, simple, and useful!
